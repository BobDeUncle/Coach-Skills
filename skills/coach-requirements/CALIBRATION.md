# Phase 2: calibration

Test the Phase 1 rules on real clients, then mock up the review queue. Every disagreement is a finding: it shows where the written rules and his judgement differ.

## A. Colour test

1. Get a real client list and a real set of check-ins (this week's, or the most recent full week) by the routes written in topic 2. Use the whole list if he has the time; otherwise at least 15 clients, including at least two of each colour and anyone he'd call borderline.
2. For each client, apply **only the written rules** from topic 3. Don't use his opinion yet. Record your colour and the rule that decided it.
3. Show him your calls in batches of about five: client, your colour, the deciding rule. Ask for his colour.
4. Where he disagrees, find out why. Then decide with him:
   - **Rule change**: edit the topic 3 rules, and note the change and the client that caused it under **Rule changes** in the calibration section.
   - **Coach's call**: a genuine judgement case. Record what made it one.
   - **Data problem**: the data was missing or wrong. Record it under topic 2.
5. After any rule change, recheck earlier clients that the change could affect.

## B. Queue mock-up

1. Take two or three orange or red clients from the test. Show each as a **client card** built to the topic 4 spec, in the queue order he chose.
2. Ask what to add, drop, move or reword. Have him tick one off and override one to see that the flow feels right.
3. Draft the end-of-run summary for the test set.
4. Record every change he asks for and update topic 4.

## Completion criterion

Every client in the test set has your colour, his colour, and (where they differ) a resolution. All rule changes are written back into topic 3. The client card and summary have been through at least one round of his feedback, and topic 4 reflects it.
